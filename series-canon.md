# Bloodrift Series Canon

Series-wide facts that all three books must agree with. The main source is the Bloodrift Trilogy Master Series Bible (November 2026), held in the claude.ai Project. Where a manuscript and the Bible disagree, the Bible is not automatically right: log the conflict in `decision-log.md` and ask Claire.

Status key: **LOCKED** = confirmed by the Bible or by Claire. **DECISION NEEDED** = Claire to choose.
