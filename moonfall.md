# Moonfall Canon

Moonfall-specific canon for the revision. Series-wide facts are in `series-canon.md` and reserved names in `reserved-names.md`. Every Moonfall edit is checked against all three. Where the manuscript and the Series Bible disagree, the item is marked **DECISION NEEDED** until Claire confirms it.

Status key: **LOCKED** = confirmed by the Bible or by Claire. **DECISION NEEDED** = Claire to choose. **PROPOSED** = the audit's recommendation, not yet confirmed.
